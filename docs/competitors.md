# Competitors by trade

Greentek does eleven services across two fields, so there is no single rival.
In each trade, the firms to beat are the ones who do **only** that trade and
do it well. This list is grouped by service, with what each firm is good at
and what we can learn from it.

Researched 2026-11-17 from web search, directory listings (Checkatrade, Which?
Trusted Traders, INCA, MCS, Bark, FreeIndex, Houzz) and the 16 competitor
landing pages read on 2026-11-05. Directory claims (review counts,
accreditations, install numbers) are the firms' own unless stated. **Check
anything before quoting it in ads or on the site**: comparative claims must
be provable (see CLAUDE.md).

Legend: **N** = national, **R** = regional (West Midlands or Wales), **L** = local single-town firm.

## 4. Loft insulation

Mostly ECO4 / Great British Insulation Scheme installers who fit it free, plus
general builders. Rivals: the ECO-funded installers (the firms on the scheme
lists) and the energy suppliers' schemes. **What to learn:** people who
qualify for free loft insulation will not pay us. The page should say who does
not qualify, or offer to check.

## 10. Full home renovation and 11. Living room improvements

Mostly general builders and design-and-build firms (the same as 8–9) plus
interior firms. Few firms do the energy upgrade and the building work
together: **this is Greentek's strongest point**, e.g. "insulate, heat pump
and renovate with one team, one survey, one guarantee".

## 12. Commercial planned maintenance

Not researched in depth. The rivals are facilities management firms (national
FM firms such as Mitie and OCS; regional building maintenance contractors).
Usually won through tenders and frameworks, not search ads.

---

## Where Greentek can win

1. **One team for energy + building.** Most rivals do one trade. Few can do the insulation, heat pump and the renovation around it.
2. **Local and in-house** against the national firms' partner installers.
3. **Reviews are the gap.** The local rivals that win have 50+ reviews in one trade. Ask every finished heat pump and solar customer for a review (only 4 reviews now, none heat pump).
4. **Badges that matter per trade:** MCS (energy), Gas Safe (heating), SWIGA/INCA (EWI), FMB (building), Which? Trusted Trader (all trades). TrustMark was removed from the site on 2026-11-17.
5. **Show prices.** Boxt, Octopus and E.ON show a price. A typical price after the grant (heat pumps) and "from" prices on every service would close the gap.
